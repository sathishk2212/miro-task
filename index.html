Task 1

Notes

My approach to managing time and deadlines is to break the work into smaller priorities A, B, C, D, E, and so on.

A is always the primary objective: delivering a video that is good enough to communicate the intended message and fulfil its purpose. B, C, D, and E are the additional creative elements and visual enhancements that elevate the overall quality of the work.

To be transparent about my time management and pacing, I followed the same approach for this video. I was able to complete the primary objective and work up to B, the Polaroid effect highlighting the hero in the image. The remaining elements would have been additional enhancements rather than essentials to the core deliverable.

  • Sound design: More detailed and intentional sound design could make the video feel more engaging and polished.
  • Foreground separation: If we had video footage instead of static images, we could roto out the foreground elements and create a much more immersive, layered composition.
  • Depth and visual effects: Additional depth, subtle parallax, and other visual treatments could add more dimension and make the overall experience visually richer.

Ultimately, my priority is to make sure the core objective is delivered within the deadline, while using the remaining time to progressively elevate the creative execution.
